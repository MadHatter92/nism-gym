How to Become a SEBI-Registered Research Analyst – A Fulfilling Career in Financial Markets

Last updated: 29 October 2025 (IST)

By Pranshumaan Singh

If you love analyzing businesses, tracking market trends, or helping others make smarter investment decisions, a career as a SEBI-Registered Research Analyst (RA) could be your calling.

With over a decade in finance—spanning investment banking, corporate strategy, and M&A—I’ve seen firsthand how this profession opens doors to exciting opportunities. In this post, I’ll break down everything you need to know about becoming a Research Analyst in India, the new SEBI reforms, and why this can be one of the most fulfilling paths in financial markets today.

Who Is a Research Analyst?

A Research Analyst studies companies, industries, and market trends to provide investment insights and recommendations. Imagine a company planning to launch a new product. The Research Analyst assesses its financials, competition, and market conditions to predict whether the launch could drive growth. These findings are then shared with clients—individual investors, fund managers, or institutions—to support informed investment decisions.

In essence, a Research Analyst is a financial detective—someone who connects data, patterns, and human behavior to help investors see the bigger picture.

How to Become a SEBI-Registered Research Analyst

SEBI has laid out a clear framework to ensure that Research Analysts maintain professional and ethical standards. Here’s how to get started:

1) Educational Qualifications

  • Minimum qualification: Graduation in any field.
  • Certification: Clear the NISM Series XV (Research Analyst) certification to gain the technical and compliance foundations required for the role.

2) Application Process

  • Fill out Form A and submit it to SEBI with your qualification proof, certification, and other required documents.
  • SEBI now follows a deposit system (in place of a net worth requirement), reducing the financial entry barrier for new analysts.

3) Fees

  • Application Fee: ₹5,000 (individuals/partnerships) or ₹50,000 (companies)
  • Registration Fee: ₹10,000 (individuals/partnerships) or ₹5,00,000 (companies)

4) Part-Time Registration

You can register as a part-time Research Analyst while continuing other employment—just disclose this to clients. You can serve up to 75 clients at a time. Once approved, SEBI issues a Certificate of Registration, making you an officially recognized Research Analyst.

SEBI’s Recent Reforms: What’s New?

  • Lower Barriers: Graduate eligibility and deposits instead of net worth criteria.
  • Part-Time Flexibility: Operate alongside other professions, with disclosures.
  • AI Transparency: If you use AI/algorithms in research, disclose this to clients.
  • Fee Freedom: Greater flexibility to set fee models suited to client needs.

These reforms signal SEBI’s intent to professionalize the space while welcoming new talent into India’s growing financial ecosystem.

Who Should Consider This Career?

  • Passionate Learners: If markets and analysis excite you, this is your playground.
  • Young Professionals: Start part-time alongside your day job.
  • Experienced Professionals: Ideal for those with finance, analytics, or business backgrounds.
  • Finance Enthusiasts: Even newcomers can build a rewarding path with the right guidance.

Ready to Start?

A SEBI-registered Research Analyst isn’t just another certification—it’s a gateway to a meaningful career in finance. With SEBI’s reforms, it’s the perfect time to begin.
