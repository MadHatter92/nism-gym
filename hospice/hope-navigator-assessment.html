Before we begin…

This quick assessment is designed for hospice leaders and clinical teams who want a brutally honest view of how tight their admission and eligibility workflows really are.

  • 10 focused questions – one per screen.
  • 2–3 minutes to complete, no preparation needed.
  • Instant readiness score with a simple interpretation.

Answer as you are today, not how you’d like things to be. The more candid you are, the more useful your score will be.

No PHI is collected. This is a directional tool, not a formal compliance review.

1. How standardized is your referral & intake tracking process?

Choose the option that best describes your organization.

2. How confident are you in your documentation of hospice eligibility?

Think about clinical notes, supporting documentation, and survey readiness.

3. How structured is your physician certification and recertification process?

Focus on timeliness, completeness, and tracking.

4. How well coordinated is your interdisciplinary team (IDT) around new admissions?

Consider communication between nursing, social work, chaplaincy, and medical staff.

5. How consistent is your communication with families at the point of admission?

Think about expectations, services, hospice philosophy, and after-hours support.

6. How mature is your symptom assessment and documentation at admission?

Consider tools like pain scales, symptom inventories, and initial care plans.

7. How effectively do you use your EHR or digital tools for the admission workflow?

Think beyond data storage – focus on workflow support and visibility.

8. How prepared do you feel for an external audit focused on admission & eligibility?

Imagine a surveyor opening 10 random charts from the last 60 days.

9. How strong is your staff training around Medicare hospice regulations and documentation?

Consider onboarding, refreshers, and practical, case-based training.

10. How clearly defined is your after-hours escalation and first-72-hours follow-up process?

Think about symptom flares, urgent calls, and rapid stabilization.
